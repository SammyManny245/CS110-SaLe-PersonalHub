## 1. Mission

We are making something unique that people would be afraid to try: a game where you wake up as a mind in a coma, with no menu and no UI telling you what to do. We agree on the story first, then the mechanics, then the code.

## 2. Team lead

**Lexxie** is the team lead. The lead listens to everyone's expertise, makes the final call on direction and scope, and records the reason. Members can ask questions and challenge decisions with evidence.

## 4. Shared mindset

- We decide as a team on mission, engine and scope before assigning work.
- We know the full backstory, even the parts the player never sees.
- We judge ideas, not people. We never put a person down.
- Everyone completes their own individual work. This contract does not transfer anyone's grading to a teammate.

## 5. Communication

- Main channel: class Discord (CS110) and the DufAyzO group chat.
- Files and code: team GitHub. Whiteboard photos and notes go to Discord.
- We reply within [24 hours] and tell the team early if we are stuck or will miss something.
- We compare results at class and share progress in the group chat before each deadline.

## 6. Work and deadlines

- Paulina tracks tasks and deadlines; David records decisions.
- Each task has one owner and a due date.
- If someone is blocked, they say so in the group chat and ask for help rather than waiting.
- Before changing shared code, we commit a checkpoint to Git so nothing is lost.

## 7. Use of AI

AI is on our roster as a team member. We say when we used it, we check what it produces, and we are responsible for the final work.

## 8. Decisions and disagreements

1. Discuss it in the group chat or at class and show evidence.
2. If we still disagree, the lead decides and writes down the reason.
3. If someone feels the problem is bigger than that, they can talk to Dr. Stalbert.

## 9. If someone leaves or does not contribute

- We first check in with the person and offer help.
- If the work still is not done, the team may reassign it and note who did the earlier work.
- Earlier work and credit stay with the person who did it, even if they leave.
